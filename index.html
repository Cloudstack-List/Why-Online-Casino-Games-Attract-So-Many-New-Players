What Should New Players Learn First?

Understanding RTP and House Edge

Two terms frequently appear when people research casino games: return to player and house edge.

Return to player, commonly called RTP, refers to a theoretical percentage associated with a game over a very large number of plays. It should not be interpreted as a guaranteed return during one session.

For example, if a game displays a particular RTP percentage, that does not mean every player will receive that exact percentage during an individual session. Actual results can vary considerably.

House edge refers to the mathematical advantage built into a game over the long term. Understanding these concepts can help you evaluate game information more realistically.

These figures are not promises of individual results. They are mathematical concepts that describe expected outcomes across a very large number of rounds.

Responsible Habits Matter

Convenience can make digital gaming easy to access, which is why personal limits are important. Before starting a session, decide how much time and money you are prepared to dedicate to entertainment.
